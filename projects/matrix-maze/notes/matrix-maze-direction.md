# Matrix-Maze Music Direction

## Tone targets

- Claustrophobic, mechanical, forward-driving.
- "You are being watched" energy, not full jump-scare intensity.
- Supports concentration while increasing pulse.

## Practical constraints

- Avoid dense melodic leads in the core loop.
- Keep low end tight and controlled to avoid masking SFX.
- Use sparse tonal stabs and filtered textures for dread.

## Arrangement behavior

- Base loop should run for long stretches without fatigue.
- Insert short 8-16 cycle variations when:
  - player enters tougher maze section
  - chase mechanic appears
  - near-miss or pressure spike events occur

## Suggested in-game mapping (later integration)

- calm exploration: base drum+bass only
- elevated threat: add hats/ghost percussion and texture
- active chase: trigger break intensity and tonal stabs
- post-chase recovery: remove top layers, keep pulse
